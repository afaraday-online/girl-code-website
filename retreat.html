healing entered the chat.

you've stepped into your softer, more holistic era. but somewhere along the way, life got a bit serious. a bit... boring. it doesn't have to be.

no bar crawl.
no small talk.

you've done the group holidays. but late nights and surface-level energy aren't really your thing anymore. maybe you've hung up your disco boots and stepped into your healing chapter.

and making friends as an adult is hard, especially after a big lifestyle change. most retreats either lack depth or don't feel welcoming to women who are conscious, spiritual, but still oh-so very human. inner work can feel lonely.

well, not anymore. just real women, living, dancing and belly laughing together.

what we'll get up to.

each day is loosely guided by an element: earth, water, fire, air and ether, woven through our ceremonies, yoga flows and the little moments in between.

move

cacao, yoga + somatic dance

cacao circles, element-inspired yoga flows and dancing like nobody's watching (they are, and they're joining in).

rest

poolside lounging

sun on your skin, a book you won't finish and laughter that makes your stomach hurt.

eat

chef meals + chaotic kitchen energy

half-board catering from our chef, plus a little diy chaotic kitchen energy where we cook together as a collective.

feel

the natural high

letting your guard down for the first time in a while, with women who get it.

the villa.

an 18th-century farmhouse tucked away in the spanish countryside, about 60 minutes from barcelona airport.

  • 7 art-filled bedrooms, each with its own bathroom
  • a saltwater pool and shaded daybeds
  • a light-filled wellbeing studio for our circles and yoga
  • indoor and outdoor dining, and a fire pit for late-night chats

share a room with a friend, or leave it to me to pair you with someone who'll end up being your new bestie.

not your average retreat.

this isn't a yoga retreat. it isn't a deep-dive healing immersive either. it's a joyful, subtly transformational space where you can show up exactly as you are, go at your own pace, connect, and just be yourself.

wellbeing isn't a matching set. it's being fully, messily human with women who get it.

your host

hi, i'm Alex.

i'm not a master, a teacher or a healer. i'll sit alongside you, guide you, and be part of your journey.

i'm a certified space holder and cacao practitioner, and i work with ceremonial cacao, nervous system regulation, journaling and meditation. most of all, i want you to feel the freedom to be your full, weird self, and to know what it's like to truly belong in sisterhood.
