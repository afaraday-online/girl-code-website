about

it started with a group chat.

in 2025 i ran something called girls day: three low-key meet-ups in london for women who wanted to meet new people.

by the end, 30+ women were in a group chat together. some had come along, some just wanted in. strangers became friends, and some of them are still friends today.

that chat was the seed for girl code. a place for women to find their people, talk about the stuff we don't get to say out loud, and do fun things together without anyone having to perform.

wellbeing isn't a matching set. it's being fully, messily human with women who get it.

the founder

hi, i'm Alex.

a london girl with a mediterranean soul. spent years living in ibiza, travelling, and now starting over in alicante. starting again so many times has shown me how hard it can be to make friends as an adult, and how much we need our girls.

i used to be a big party girl. these days i host retreats and cacao circles, but still love good wine, great food, the beach, and a very good night out. i'm weird, i'm woo + i swear - you've been warned.

girl code is the thing i needed. and now it's yours too.
