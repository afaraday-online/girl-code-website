the small print

terms + conditions.

the agreement between you and girl code. last updated 26 october 2026.

These terms are the agreement between Alexandra Faraday, trading as girl code ("girl code", "we", "us"), and you ("you", the member or guest). They cover everything girl code runs: membership, online sessions, in-person meet-ups and events, retreats, and the girl code group chat.

They exist so everyone knows what to expect, and so girl code stays a respectful, safe space for all of us. By joining as a member, booking an event or joining the group chat, you agree to these terms.

1. who can join

By joining or booking, you confirm that you:

  • are 18 or over
  • identify as a woman. girl code is a women's space, and trans women are welcome. A few events may be open to everyone (like our gals & gays night); when they are, the event listing will say so clearly
  • are physically, mentally and emotionally able to take part in the activities you book
  • understand that girl code is a community, not therapy. Our sessions are not a substitute for medical, psychological or therapeutic support

2. your health and wellbeing

You are responsible for your own physical, mental, emotional and spiritual wellbeing before, during and after anything you take part in. Alex is not a medical professional.

Some sessions may not be right for you if you:

  • are in acute mental health crisis, or are working through trauma without support
  • have a physical condition that could be made worse by movement, yoga, breathwork, ceremonial cacao or other activities on offer

If you're unsure, please speak to a qualified professional and message us before booking.

Before a session or event, tell us about any health conditions, injuries, allergies or mental health considerations that could affect your participation. We'll keep this private.

Cacao: if you have a heart condition or a sensitivity to fermented foods, or you take antidepressants, check with a medical professional before drinking ceremonial cacao. Taking part is always your choice.

Retreats and events abroad: you need suitable travel insurance, and we may ask for your policy details before you arrive.

3. the girl code way

Our circles are spaces to share, to witness and to be witnessed. They are not for advice-giving, coaching or fixing. Wherever we meet, online or in person, you agree to:

  • keep what others share private. What's said in the group stays in the group
  • speak from your own experience ("I" statements), not generalisations
  • not give advice unless someone asks for it, and never diagnose anyone
  • respect boundaries around touch, personal space, identities and different backgrounds
  • arrive on time, and understand that late arrivals may not be let in

We can remove anyone whose behaviour is unsafe, disrespectful, disruptive or harmful to others, without a refund. This includes verbal, physical or emotional harm, and it applies to events, online sessions and the group chat.

4. membership

  • Price: membership is £15 a month, taken automatically each month by card. If you pay from outside the UK, you'll see the amount in your own currency at checkout.
  • Founding rate: founding members keep £15 a month for as long as they stay members.
  • Minimum term: membership has a 2-month minimum. After that, you can cancel anytime.
  • Cooling-off: you can cancel within 14 days of first joining for a full refund, minus the value of any member sessions or discounts you've already used.
  • Cancelling: email hello@joingirlcode.com or use the link in your payment receipt. Your membership runs until the end of the month you've paid for.
  • Rejoining: if you cancel, you lose your current rate. If you rejoin later, it's at whatever the price is then.
  • Missed payments: if a payment fails and isn't fixed within 7 days, your membership may be paused.

The members' code gives you members' rates on events. It's for your own ticket only, once per event. Please don't share it. If a non-member books with it, we may cancel that booking or ask them to pay the difference.

Bring a friend: if a friend you bring to an event joins as a member, you get 1 month free and she pays just £10 for her first month. This is for members only.

5. event bookings, refunds and transfers

These apply to all paid events, online and in person.

  • Tickets are non-refundable.
  • Can't make it? With at least 7 days' notice, you can move your ticket to another date (if spaces are available) or give it to a friend who meets these terms. Email hello@joingirlcode.com and we'll confirm.
  • Within 7 days of the event, we can't offer refunds or transfers. This also applies to no-shows, late arrivals and changes of mind.
  • Deposits and instalments (for retreats): payments must be made by the dates given when you book. If they aren't, your place may be released and a refund may not be possible.
  • If we cancel or postpone an event, you can choose a full refund or a place on the new date. Refunds are issued within 7 days.
  • If we change an event (activities, venue or co-hosts), we'll let you know as early as we can. If the change means you can no longer come, we'll offer you a transfer or a refund.

6. online sessions

  • Online yoga, circles and rituals happen on Zoom. You need a Zoom account, a stable internet connection and a quiet, private space where you can take part safely.
  • Sessions start on time. If you're more than 10 minutes late, you may not be let in, so you don't disrupt the group. Missed sessions aren't refunded.
  • Keep your camera on during circles if you can. Please don't record, screenshot or share the session.
  • If something goes wrong on our side (like the music not playing), we'll share a Spotify playlist or a replacement instead. If we have to cancel, section 5 applies.

7. the group chat

The girl code group chat is for members. To keep it a good place to be:

  • be kind. No bullying, harassment, hate or discrimination of any kind
  • don't screenshot or share other people's messages or photos outside the chat
  • no selling, promoting your business or sending unsolicited DMs to sell something, unless we've said it's okay
  • no explicit, violent or illegal content

We can remove messages, and remove anyone who breaks these rules from the chat and from membership, without a refund. Access to the chat ends when your membership ends.

8. photos, video and recordings

  • We may take photos and video at events for girl code's website, socials and marketing.
  • We may record parts of sessions for training and our own records. We never use audio from circles publicly, because what's shared there is private.
  • We'll always check with you before using a close-up or vulnerable photo or clip of you publicly.
  • Don't want to be in photos or videos? Email hello@joingirlcode.com before the event, or tell us on the day. You can change your mind at any time, and we'll respect it.
  • Please don't take photos or videos of other members without asking them first.

9. taking part and responsibility

  • Every activity is voluntary. You take part at your own choice and risk, and you can sit anything out.
  • If ceremonial cacao is offered, you're responsible for deciding whether to drink it, and for understanding what it is.
  • You're responsible for your own belongings at events.
  • girl code is not liable for any injury, loss or damage arising from taking part, except where caused by our negligence, or where the law says otherwise.

10. your details, changes and contact

  • Your details: we use your name, email and booking details to run your membership and bookings and to send you girl code emails. You can unsubscribe from emails at any time. We never sell your details. Any health information you share is kept private and only used to keep you safe.
  • Changes to these terms: we may update these terms from time to time. We'll tell members about any important changes by email before they apply.
  • Contact: hello@joingirlcode.com
